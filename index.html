ALPINE EMPIRE

Drawing on a decade of experience in creating immersive, believable, and enjoyable game environments, I took a risk and established Alpine Empire. My professional project's goal is to create cutting-edge 3D environment art and game art tools, as well as to provide mentorship for the new generation of world builders.
WHO IS ALPINE EMPIRE

Matthias
Patscheider

Born and raised in the narrow, picturesque valleys of the Alps, among rugged peaks, historic farmhouses, and medieval castles — a landscape that shaped a lifelong passion for crafting believable worlds.

Alpine Empire is built on a decade of game development experience, from small startups to AAA titles, shaping worlds, levels, and pipelines at every stage of production. As an Environment Artist, I've contributed to titles such as Alan Wake 2, FBC Firebreak, Black Mirror, and Crossfire X, plus VR training projects.

World-building sits at the heart of it all: I research and understand environments to create spaces that feel authentic. I pair that with building workflows and tools that push what's possible, always in close collaboration with teams, directors, leads, and producers.

Open Source & Knowledge Sharing. I use the best tool for the job, favoring open source and European alternatives where they hold up.

Skills

What I build, and how I work.

HARD SKILLS
Level Art & Design
Building gameplay-driven levels and modular kits, with combat, metrics, and player guidance in mind, from first blockout to final polish. I managed and shipped key levels for several games, including Alan Wake 2's Dark Place.
Asset Creation
Crafting modular architectural kits, PBR materials, hard-surface machinery, props and foliage, I can create highest quality assets and materials if needed, preferably using non-destructive workflows.
Proceduralism
A systemic approach to content creation without compromising art directability. Creating bespoke solutions or a generic library of reusable tools with Houdini or Blender Geometry Nodes.
SOFT SKILLS
Positive & Solution-Oriented
Open, straightforward, and solution-oriented. I am proactively seeking feedback and staying constructive whenever a challenge shows up.
Collaboration & Innovation
I work closely with directors, producers, and teams, staying open to new tools and pipelines, proposing better workflows and making sure the work aligns with the creative vision.
Ownership & Responsibility
I am a reliable, independent teammate who can take full ownership of levels and assets from start to finish, managing time and quality to meet deadlines.
