SIMPLE Camera Manager

One panel. Every camera. Full control. Centralize your camera settings, batch rendering, and quick switching — all from the 3D View.
01
CAMERA OVERVIEW

One Panel,
Every Camera

See every camera in your scene in one place. Sort the list by Active First, Collection, Focal Length, Render Slot, Resolution, or Background Image — then filter to visible-only or render-selected cameras.

✓ Adjust every setting in a spreadsheet-style panel
✓ All settings at one glance
02
BATCH RENDERING

Render Every Camera,
One Click

Render multiple static cameras with different settings in one go — with visible progress in the render view, not a black screen until it's done.

✓ Only batch renderer I'm aware of that shows the render update live
✓ Doesn't rely on misusing stereoscopic settings or camera markers
✓ Each camera keeps its own resolution, exposure, and settings
✓ Uses the current scene frame for every camera in the batch
03
CUSTOM RESOLUTIONS

Every Camera,
Its Own Resolution

Unlike Blender's scene-based resolution, assign resolutions directly per camera — ideal for social posts, marketing renders, and detailed shots without touching scene settings each time.

The Resolution Overwrite toggle per camera prevents unwanted resolution changes when switching.
04
RENDER SLOTS

Never Overwrite
a Render Again

Blender writes every render to the same slot by default — silently overwriting your last render, no matter which camera triggered it. Assign a dedicated render slot to each camera so every shot stays put, ready to compare side by side.
05
DOLLY ZOOM

Find the Perfect
Focal Length

Interactively perform a dolly zoom — the camera moves closer or further from the subject while the zoom angle adjusts to keep the subject the same size.

✓ Reference Plane Gizmo defines what should always stay in focus
✓ Modal hotkeys adjust the dolly zoom by distance or by focal length
06
BACKGROUND IMAGES

Model From
Real-World Photos

Manage viewport background images to recreate real-world objects and scenes. Toggle between images, adjust opacity, and match the camera's focal length to the reference with the dolly zoom.

Project: Cotton Spinning Mule Modeling ↗
07
PASSEPARTOUT OVERLAY

Know a Camera's State,
Right in the Viewport

A camera's passepartout frame tints in the 3D View, so you always know what you're looking at before you touch a dial.

✓ Blue frame — camera transform is locked
✓ Red frame — camera is tied to the current viewport view
✓ Both colors fully customizable in preferences
