TRANSPARENCY & CONTROL

✓ Total transparency — no hidden processes, no surprises
✓ Full control — adjustable, reusable presets for predictable results

02
ONE-CLICK BATCH EXPORT

Every Collection,
One Click

Export multiple collections or scenes in seconds, with a failed-status indicator so you immediately see what needs fixing. Toggle between a compact overview and a full detailed view.

✓ Outliner sync — selecting a collection highlights it both ways
✓ Interactive warnings — click one for a full explanation
✓ Persistent settings, saved directly in your .blend file

PRE-EXPORT PIPELINE

Clean Meshes,
Without the Cleanup

Triangulate meshes and apply scale & rotation before export — without permanently modifying your scene. Every pre-export operation is restored right after, and can be set per collection with per-collection overrides.
